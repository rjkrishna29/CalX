---

## ✨ Features

CalX is packed with features to make your calculations seamless and efficient.

#### **Core Functionality**
-   ✅ **Basic Arithmetic:** Perform addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division.
-   🔢 **Advanced Operations:** Includes support for percentages and parentheses for complex calculations.
-   📋 **Copy & Paste:** Easily copy results or inputs with a single tap or a long-press.
-   📜 **Calculation History:** Access your last 10 calculations anytime from the history view.
-   ⬅️ **Backspace & Clear:** Effortlessly correct mistakes with a backspace button or clear all inputs with 'AC'.

#### **Powerful Converters**
-   💻 **Number System Converter:** Seamlessly convert numbers between **Binary**, **Decimal**, **Octal**, and **Hexadecimal**.
-   📏 **Length Converter:** Convert various units of length with accurate results.
-   🗺️ **Area Converter:** Quickly convert between different units of area.

#### **User Experience**
-   🌓 **Light & Dark Modes:** Switch between themes for a comfortable viewing experience in any lighting.
-   🎨 **Monochromatic Icon:** Supports themed icons for a clean, minimalist look on your home screen (Android 13+).
-   📱 **Modern UI:** A clean, intuitive interface that is easy to navigate.

## 🚀 Getting Started

### **Prerequisites**
-   An Android device running **Android 9 (Pie)** or higher.

### **Installation**
1.  Navigate to the [**Releases**](https://github.com/rjkrishna29/CalX_apk/releases) page.
2.  Download the latest `CalX.apk` file.
3.  Open the downloaded file on your Android device and follow the on-screen instructions to install the app.
    *(You may need to enable "Install from unknown sources" in your device settings.)*
